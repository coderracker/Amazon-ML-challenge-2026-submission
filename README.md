# Amazon-ML-challenge-2026-submission
Large-Scale Business Entity Resolution

EDA was performed in Jupyter, final challenge submission in Google Collab.
Dataset: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1VMdlOzpcUQCZ-_i5ZQWhzrwQHSid-ko-?usp=drive_link
This was my submission for the amazon ML challenge 2026.

In this folder, I have performed basic EDA at EDA.ipynb , to look at the training data.
I then made a submission, that was somewhat good, but because of optimizations to reduce processing time from 30-40 hours to 10 hours and shifting the data into the disc rather than RAM in collab, There were a lot of optimizations, that also meant reducing Recall heavily. To make atleast a submission, I did that.

I then started working on a more optimized version(the file is the uploaded one), in the experiment file. I tried variety of blocking combinations to maximize recall while reducing overlapping and reducing overall time and memory usage, and keeping it generalized.
I also worked on a newer model, based on the new features generated by the blocks.(all in amazon_ml_10k_artifacts)
I then tried a pipeline, that also used disc ram, but should have been faster and given much better results. But since my candidate generation pair was too high, even after good blocking strategies, I then reduced the candidate pairs to top 3 candidates, and increased the chunk size to use the unused RAM, since the chunks were loading, processing and unloading on the RAM from disc.
The strategy did bring down my time from 40 hrs of collab to 6-7 hours, but unfortunately I did not have any time for submission under the challenge.
